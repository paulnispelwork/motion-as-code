# Method: one film at a time, with a person watching

The films that worked went through several rounds of a person watching a playing draft and giving notes (one had 7 rounds in a day). This process builds that in. Commands are in [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md); rules are in [CRAFT.md](CRAFT.md).

## Checkpoints

At each checkpoint, stop and wait for the reviewer (the person you work for). While you wait, only do work that doesn't depend on the answer.

| | The reviewer gets | The reviewer does |
|---|---|---|
| **C0 Treatment** | One page at the top of `PLAN.md` ([step 3](#3-treatment-c0)) | Approves or redirects |
| **C1 Hero moment** | The key 6–10 s, playing, with sound | Watches, gives notes |
| **C2 First full draft** | The first whole film (usually the first ad), playing, with sound, plus the self-check and a side-by-side with a reference film | Notes on timing, easing, pacing, copy |
| **C3 Notes rounds** | A new playing draft and a list of what changed | Round 2, sometimes 3 |
| **C4 Final file** | The full-quality MP4 after file QA | Watches it on a phone, muted first. Only then is it done |

Later films in the same set start at C2. A film with a new idea or a new hero moment goes through C1 again.

After each checkpoint the same session carries on. If it gets long, write the state (the reviewer's notes, where you are, the next step) to `NOTES.md`; the reviewer then starts a fresh session with the kickoff prompt and "continue from NOTES.md".

## Steps

### 1. Brief

The blanks are those in [prompts/new-film.md](prompts/new-film.md): what to make, the goal, where it runs, the audience, the offer, how the pieces differ, the language and form of address, the sound and the deadline. Ask once, in one message, for any that are empty. The one default: the form of address follows the brand's site.

### 2. Research

Collect the product before any idea exists.

- **Folder:** use `films/<name>/`, with `make.sh` copied from an earlier film and edited.
- **The real product:** screenshots of the key flow (entry → key action → result) into `research/`. If there's no usable product imagery, ask. Never replace the product with a metaphor.
- **What already wins:** if the brand runs ads, read their best-performing ones for hooks and voice.
- **`brand/BRAND.md`:**
  - colours from the site CSS, fonts from `@font-face`, the logo from the header SVG, and the logo's construction (the best source of a device);
  - the claims you may use, verbatim with source URLs, and the claims to avoid.

  The live site wins over ads and old notes.
- **`## Brand` in `PLAN.md`:** what it is in one sentence; who it's for; what sets it apart; the register (sober or playful, premium or practical); the visual hook for the first second.

### 3. Treatment (C0)

One page under `## Treatment` at the top of `PLAN.md`. No code before the reviewer approves it.

```
Offer and audience:  the offer; the audience call-out per ad ("Running ads on Meta or LinkedIn?"), on screen in the first second
Idea:                one sentence
Films:               which, format, length, placement; one feature per ad
Product on screen:   which screens to rebuild (screenshot paths), where each is the hero
Hero object:         the one shape or object that carries the film, and what it becomes
Palette and type:    ≤ 3 grounds + 1 accent; 2 type roles; headline sizes
Mechanics:           which proven mechanics (CRAFT §2); anything new is flagged
Sound:               the proposed source; tempo; the drop; the payoff
Beat table:          per scene: beats, copy, product, camera beat, the named transition out
Not slop:            what this film will never show
Frame cost:          the heaviest scene, and how it stays ≤ 25 ms
```


### 4. Sound and grid

- **The reviewer picks the source at C0:** a licensed track, Suno (paid plan, generated by hand), the synth as a stand-in, or silent. The synth ships at C4 only if the reviewer accepts it; otherwise the track is in `music/` by C3.
- With a track, fit it first ([RUNBOOK: sound](RUNBOOK.md#sound)). The track's BPM and drop set the grid.
- Every event goes on the grid: cuts and big moves on the loud bars, rest in the quiet bars, the payoff on the drop.

### 5. Hero moment (C1)

Build the key 6–10 s on an empty page on `engine.js`, using the proven mechanics and the real product UI, with sound. Get it playing in real time on the right beats before you polish anything.

### 6. First full draft (C2)

Build the first whole film. Run the [self-check](CRAFT.md#self-check). Put a frame of yours next to a reference film at the same story moment, and write one honest paragraph on where yours is weaker.

### 7. Notes rounds (C3)

Write the reviewer's notes verbatim into `NOTES.md`, then work through them:
- a timing pass: holds, hits, springs, camera weight;
- then a finishing pass: grain, glow, motion blur, grade.

"This doesn't work" means you replace the idea; don't polish it.

### 8. Final (C4)

Render at full quality only after the last notes, with `make.sh` ([RUNBOOK](RUNBOOK.md#build-and-render)). Send the MP4 and wait for the reviewer.

### 9. Deliver

Put together `deliver/`, the poster frame, `share-copy.txt` and the music rights ([RUNBOOK: deliver](RUNBOOK.md#deliver)). Add what you learned to CRAFT (craft) or RUNBOOK (tools).

## What to hand over at a checkpoint

- From C1 on, something to **play**:
  - the `?play` link;
  - a 540p preview MP4 with sound ([RUNBOOK: previews](RUNBOOK.md#previews)).
- Which checkpoint this is, what changed, and at most 3 open questions.
- From C2 on: the self-check numbers and the side-by-side.
- Not contact sheets as the main artefact, and not long reports.

Before C2 there's no render to measure. At C0 and C1, check the facts against `BRAND.md`, the beat table (everything on the grid, read time budgeted, a named transition out of every scene), and 0.1-beat frame strips across the hero moment's transitions.

## Who judges what

| The agent checks, with numbers | Only the reviewer judges |
|---|---|
| Safe area, type size, read time, overlaps, `seek` purity (`qa_page.js`) | Whether the easing and camera feel weighted or floaty |
| Punctuation, holds, hits, grade, palette (`measure.py`) | Whether the timing lands, and whether music and cuts agree |
| Product share and size, ad length, the hook in the first second | Whether the product looks convincing and desirable |
| Frame time ≤ 25 ms | Whether the idea is strong and the copy sells |
| Facts against `BRAND.md` and the site | Whether it looks like every other film |
| File QA: format, colour, loudness (`qa_file.py`) | Whether it's done |

## Working as an agent

- **Keep the state in files:** `PLAN.md` for the treatment and beat tables, `NOTES.md` for the reviewer's notes and the next step.
- **Images:** look at nothing wider than 1600 px. Full-resolution crops are allowed for edges and type. Never read raw QA logs into context.
- **Renders:** one at a time. Never edit `engine.js`, `render.js` or `motion.js` while a render runs.
